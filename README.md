# IP-38 Estimating fish biomass in the Baltic Sea with acoustic and trawl data

_Clupea harengus_ (Atlantic Herring), _Sprattus sprattus_ (Sprat) and _Gasterosteus aculeatus_ (Threespined Stickleback) are dominant fish species in the Baltic Sea. They have high ecological and commercial value. Estimating their abundance is therefore an important practical question. To this end, a joint international efforts – the Baltic International Acoustic Survey (BIAS) – conducting both acoustic and trawling surveys are deployed annually. Currently, to convert survey data into the fish abundance estimates, the StoX software is employed to analyse data produced by BIAS. This method does not measure uncertainty of the abundance estimates and produces the estimates in spatially aggregated manner for the ICES Statistical Rectangles. The statistical rectangles are large and the borders between them are arbitrary compared to environmental variability in the Baltic Sea. Moreover, the BIAS sampling locations within one rectangle may be spatially and temporally unbalanced, potentially creating bias to the biomass estimates of that rectangle. Both acoustic and trawling samples are also subject to observational errors, resulting in noisy estimates, which are not accounted for by StoX software. To improve the estimates, we developed a fully Bayesian framework for estimating fish abundances by integrating the complementary data products of BIAS and spatiotemporally explicit covariates into model-based estimate over the whole Baltic Sea.  

**This work is ongoing and code is not yet available.**
